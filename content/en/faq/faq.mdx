## Is Beyond These Stars Out?

Yes. The full 1.0 released on October 16, 2026 on Steam and the Epic Games Store. Not early access, not a beta — the complete game, campaign and systems whole, with a 20% launch discount running on Steam through the launch window. The launch guide carries the full transaction details; the short version is that every usual launch-buy uncertainty resolved in the buyer's favor before checkout.

## What Is the Game About?

You build a civilization on the back of Kewa, a space whale journeying through the galaxy. Your people — the Pequeninos — need food, homes, work, education and eventually purpose, and you provide through supply chains placed thoughtfully across the whale's curved, living landscape while the journey's story unfolds around you. The complete guide covers the premise in depth; the systems pages cover the chains, the needs ladder and the whale itself.

## Wasn't It Supposed to Be Early Access?

It was originally, and then it wasn't — that is the launch's signature story. In March 2026, Balancing Monkey Games announced the game would skip its planned early access phase entirely and ship complete. October 16 is that promise kept: no roadmap to bet on, no promised features pending, the whole game on the table at purchase. It is one of the more consumer-friendly launch shapes the genre has offered, and worth knowing about because it defines what your money buys.

## Who Made It?

Balancing Monkey Games, the New Zealand studio behind Before We Leave, published by Hooded Horse — the strategy-focused publisher whose catalog has made its logo a quality signal for the genre's audience. The studio's post-launch support record on their previous game is part of the buying calculus, and it is a good record.

## Do I Need to Play Before We Leave First?

No. Beyond These Stars is a semi-sequel — same universe and people, completely standalone story and tutorial. The comparison page is written for fans of the original who want the full lineage; newcomers can ignore it entirely and start here.

## Is It Hard?

It is gently demanding, which is a different thing. Nothing in the game punishes you the way survival builders or crisis-management sims do — no disasters for sport, no fail-states that erase hours. The challenge is logistical: supply chains that hum, needs ladders climbed in order, placement that respects walking time and the whale's curves. The beginner guide sequences the opening hours so the learning curve stays a pleasure; the floor is low and the ceiling is honest optimization, which is the shape the cozy-strategy audience actually wants.

## What Platforms, and What Does It Cost?

PC, via Steam and the Epic Games Store — identical complete builds, so the store choice is preference and price, not content. Launch pricing: 20% off on Steam through the launch window, one clean edition with no tiering or cosmetic SKU maze. The live store pages are the authority on today's number; this wiki records the launch's shape (discounted window, single edition) rather than freezing prices.

## Are There Mods?

Not yet in any established sense — the game is days old at this writing, and a mod scene is something communities build, not launch features. The studio's communication channels are the live source for any official modding news; this page updates if that landscape changes.
